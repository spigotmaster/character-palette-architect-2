Colour palettes for characters, with the reasoning shown.

How to use it: choose a skin undertone and depth, tap one or more personality traits, then choose an age. All four choices are needed. Your 12-colour palette appears under step 3, with the season wheels, sliders and an explanation. Optionally, upload a sketch to try the colours on it.

The palette is applied automatically: skin from your skin tone, the biggest areas get the base colour, hair the support colour, small details alternate between three accent colours. Wrong spot? Pick a part, pick a colour, then tap that area. Works best on sketches with closed outlines.

Complete the three questions below to generate a palette first.

1. Skin tone: pick undertone and depth

Undertone: the hue beneath the skin. Not sure? Warm looks golden, peach or olive; cool looks pink, red or bluish. For a fictional character, pick what suits the design.

Depth: how light or dark the skin is.
